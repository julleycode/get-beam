# beam SEO plan, built off the Alventra playbook (translated for B2B SaaS)

Date: 15 June 2026
Product: beam (getbeam.fyi), website visitor identification plus drafted social outreach for indie and DTC founders
Competitors: RB2B, Clay, Warmly, Vector, Koala

## First principles: why the original article does not fit, and what does

The Alventra article is a local SEO playbook for home services businesses. All eight prompts optimize a Google Business Profile to win the Maps "map pack." beam has no physical location, no map pack, no Google reviews from local customers. Copying those eight prompts literally would burn time for zero return.

The one thing worth keeping is the system, not the tactics: load deep business context into Claude once, then run a repeatable prompt stack that audits competitors, finds the gaps, and hands back a ranked action plan. This document is the beam version of that stack and its output.

SEO exists to catch the person who is actively hunting for a solution. For a plumber that hunt happens in Google Maps. For beam it happens in three places: AI answer engines (a founder asks ChatGPT or Perplexity "best RB2B alternative"), comparison and alternative content (someone types "rb2b alternative"), and the communities the ICP lives in (Indie Hackers, r/SaaS, r/sales). That is where the real fight is.

## The brutal one line summary

beam is invisible. Across every buying intent query the research tested, beam appears in zero AI answers, zero listicles, zero comparison pages, zero community threads. The category's AI answers are written by roughly ten competitor blogs that rank their own product first. On top of that beam has a brand discovery problem: "beam" is a common word and getbeam.fyi competes with Beam AI, Beam Impact, getbeam.ai and others. This is a from zero position, which is bad news and good news. Bad: no existing equity. Good: there is a live, time sensitive opening that no competitor is serving.

## The live opening (act on this in days, not weeks)

RB2B removed person level resolution from its free plan on 22 January 2026 and pushed entry pricing to roughly 129 dollars a month. That dumped RB2B's old indie and bootstrapper base into the market right now, hunting for a cheaper alternative. That cohort is beam's exact ICP. There is already a fresh Indie Hackers thread, "6 RB2B Alternatives Now That the Free Plan Is Gone," that AI engines are citing for the indie founder query, and beam is not in it. This window closes as competitors fill it. It is the single highest leverage move available.

## What beam actually owns that competitors do not

Three real, defensible angles came out of the teardown:

1. The indie and solo and DTC founder segment is uncontested. Every serious competitor is priced and written for teams. Warmly starts around 700 dollars a month, Vector around 4,000 dollars a month, Clay needs a technical RevOps operator. The only "affordable" content is generic and not founder voiced. beam's positioning plus lowercase founder voice sits in genuinely empty territory.

2. "Drafts the outreach for you" answers the number one complaint about RB2B. Buyers leave RB2B because it is a raw feed to Slack: it identifies, then you do everything else. beam drafting personalized social outreach is a feature none of the top five lead with.

3. Multi platform, not LinkedIn only. RB2B and most rivals are heavily LinkedIn dependent, and LinkedIn has been actively cutting off data access. beam matching across X, Instagram and others is a resilience story. Frame it as "not dependent on one platform," not as a match rate brag (the 60 to 80 percent claim stays out of ads per brand rule).

## How this maps back to the article's eight prompts

So you can see the translation rather than trust it blindly:

- Their "GBP category audit" becomes beam's AEO audit (move 9): are you visible in the answer when the buyer asks.
- Their "competitor review teardown" becomes the comparison and alternative gap analysis (moves 3 to 5).
- Their "GBP posts and photos engine" becomes the content engine (moves 4, 7, 8) plus community presence (move 1).
- Their "load business context once" step is the only prompt that transfers directly, and it is already done: beam's context lives in memory (getbeam.fyi, ICP, competitors RB2B and Clay, lowercase voice, no fake proof, no 60 to 80 percent claim in ads).
