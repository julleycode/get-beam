## delivery notes

This only works if the opener is felt, not read. "let's be honest" has to sound like you actually mean it, a little tired of the status quo, not announcing. Shoot the first line ten times and keep the most human take. The algorithm reads a flat first three seconds as a skip and buries the video.

Pace fast through the problem (0:04 to 0:40), then slow down and warm up for "this is the future where." That tempo shift is what makes a manifesto feel like a manifesto instead of a pitch.

Keep it handheld and close. This is a confession and a mission, not a product demo. If you want one product flash, drop a two second screen recording of a real beam visitor resolving under the line "people you can actually reach" at 1:08. Optional, do not let it break the eye contact for long.

Lowercase captions, burned in, one line at a time. It has to land on mute.

Guardrails held: no match rate number on camera, the 97% is your own existing site line so it is fine, no fake names on any demo screen.
