# beam launch video, storyboard (the founder story version)

A normal, warm launch film. You tell the story of why beam exists, then show it. No skit, no laser gun. Just you, the problem you lived, and the product as the answer. This is the version that ages well and lives on your site and pinned tweet.

Runtime target: 1:30. Panels 1 to 8 (the first 44 seconds) form a complete sub 60 second cut for the For You feed. Panels 9 to 14 are the extension for the hero version. Cut point is marked.

Tone: honest, a little understated, confident without hype. Talk like you are telling one founder friend the thing you finally fixed. beam voice stays lowercase. Most people watch muted, so every panel has a burned in caption that carries the meaning alone.

Look: shoot handheld and slightly imperfect for the to camera shots, it reads as real. Clean, well lit screen recordings for the product. One warm key light on your face, soft. Shallow depth of field. Quiet ambient music that lifts at the product reveal (panel 5) and resolves at the end card.

---

## storyboard

### PANEL 01 · 0:00 to 0:04 · the hook
Shot: tight close up, you to camera, handheld, you are mid thought like the camera caught you talking.
Frame: your face fills the left two thirds, soft background, a sliver of your workspace behind.
Action: you look slightly off lens then land on it as you say the line.
On screen caption: **who was that?**
VO: "i used to watch people land on my site, read everything, and then just leave. and i never knew who they were."
Sound: room tone only, no music yet. it should feel like a confession.

### PANEL 02 · 0:04 to 0:10 · the scale of it
Shot: over your shoulder, laptop screen in focus, you out of focus in foreground.
Frame: analytics dashboard, a live visitor counter ticking up, 12, then 80, then higher.
Action: the number climbs on its own. you do nothing. that is the point.
On screen caption: **500 visitors today.**
VO: "every single day, hundreds of them showed up."
Sound: a soft pulse begins under it.

### PANEL 03 · 0:10 to 0:16 · the ache
Shot: back to you, a touch wider, you lean back in the chair.
Frame: you centered, a little defeated, half a smile because it is almost funny how bad it is.
Action: small shrug.
On screen caption: **0 of them, i actually talked to.**
VO: "and i talked to exactly none of them. no email, no signup, no name. just a number going up while i did nothing."
Sound: pulse continues.

### PANEL 04 · 0:16 to 0:24 · the turn
Shot: you sit forward, energy shifts, the realization.
Frame: lean into the lens, this is the line the whole film pivots on.
Action: you point at the camera lightly on the second sentence.
On screen caption: **not a traffic problem.**
VO: "and then it clicked. i did not have a traffic problem. i had a 'i have no idea who these people are' problem."
Sound: music holds, almost drops out for a beat before the reveal.

### PANEL 05 · 0:24 to 0:28 · the reveal
Shot: hard cut to a clean product beauty shot, beam wordmark resolving on a nice screen.
Frame: centered, lots of negative space, premium and calm.
Action: the mark settles. simple.
On screen caption: **introducing beam.**
VO: "so i built beam."
Sound: music lifts here. this is the moment.

### PANEL 06 · 0:28 to 0:36 · step one, see them
Shot: screen recording of the live visitor list, your face small in the corner circle.
Frame: a real row resolves from anonymous into a name, a company, a page title.
Action: cursor hovers the row, the details populate.
On screen caption: **01 · see them.**
VO: "beam shows you who is actually on your site. their name, their company, the page they are reading, right now."
Sound: clean UI ticks, music bed under.

### PANEL 07 · 0:36 to 0:44 · step two, find them
Shot: screen recording, a social profile match snapping into focus.
Frame: the visitor's x handle and linkedin appear next to their name.
Action: the matched profile card slides in.
On screen caption: **02 · find them.**
VO: "then it finds where they actually hang out. it matches their x handle, their linkedin, so you know exactly where to show up."
Sound: music bed continues.

> CUT POINT FOR THE SHORT VERSION. End the For You cut right after the next panel's first sentence, then jump to a two second CTA card (panel 13 plus 14). That gives you a complete 45 second video.

### PANEL 08 · 0:44 to 0:54 · step three, say hi
Shot: screen recording of the drafted reply appearing, your face back in corner.
Frame: a genuinely good, specific reply that references their real post, not a creepy "i saw you visited."
Action: the draft types itself in, you nod.
On screen caption: **03 · say hi back.**
VO: "and it writes you the reply. based on their real posts, in your voice. you read it, change a word if you want, and send it from your own account."
Sound: a soft heart floats up on screen, the beam signature moment.

### PANEL 09 · 0:54 to 1:02 · what it is not
Shot: back to you, to camera, warmer and slower now.
Frame: medium close up, you are leveling with the viewer.
Action: calm, direct.
On screen caption: **not a cold email. a hello.**
VO: "this is not a cold email that dies in spam. it is a real hello, on their feed, while they still remember being on your site."
Sound: music softens to let you talk.

### PANEL 10 · 1:02 to 1:10 · the trust beat
Shot: you, same setup, this is the objection killer.
Frame: steady, sincere.
Action: a small hand gesture, open palms, nothing to hide.
On screen caption: **it stays you.**
VO: "no bots, no fake profiles, no logging into your accounts. you send every message yourself, from native x. it stays you."
Sound: bed continues.

### PANEL 11 · 1:10 to 1:16 · the speed beat
Shot: quick screen recording, a reply going out, the heart float again.
Frame: snappy, energetic, a palette cleanser before the close.
Action: one click, sent.
On screen caption: **about 30 seconds.**
VO: "and the whole thing takes about thirty seconds."
Sound: music starts its build toward the close.

### PANEL 12 · 1:16 to 1:24 · the why
Shot: back to you, to camera, the most sincere frame of the film.
Frame: tight, eye contact, this is the line people remember.
Action: still, let it land.
On screen caption: **say hi back.**
VO: "i built beam because the people already visiting your site are the warmest leads you will ever get. you should get to say hi back."
Sound: full warm bed.

### PANEL 13 · 1:24 to 1:30 · the CTA
Shot: you, holding the energy, a small smile.
Frame: same, friendly.
Action: light, inviting, not salesy.
On screen caption: **private beta · limited spots.**
VO: "it is in private beta right now. spots are limited, and i onboard everyone myself."
Sound: bed resolves.

### PANEL 14 · 1:30 to 1:34 · the end card
Shot: clean end card, beam wordmark and tagline, your handle small underneath.
Frame: centered, calm, premium, matches panel 5.
Action: mark settles, hold, fade.
On screen caption: **beam · see who's into you. say hi back.**
VO: "beam. see who's into you. say hi back."
Sound: music button, soft end.
Note: do not put the link on the end card as a clickable expectation. the link lives in your first reply on the post. the wordmark and getbeam.fyi as plain text on screen is fine as branding.

## shot list to capture (so you shoot efficiently)

To camera, warm key light, handheld: panels 1, 3, 4, 9, 10, 12, 13. Shoot these in one sitting, same outfit, same framing, you can reorder in the edit.

Over the shoulder at the laptop: panel 2.

Screen recordings, captured clean at high resolution: panels 6, 7, 8, 11. Record these on a real or obviously demo beam account. Use demo data or your own real screen. Do not show fake names of real people.

Product beauty and end card: panels 5 and 14. These can be motion graphics done in the edit, you do not need to film them.

---

## edit and music notes

Keep cuts fast in the first 24 seconds, every panel earns its place, no lingering. Let the to camera shots breathe slightly more after the reveal.

Music: one track, quiet and a little hopeful. Near silent under the hook, pulse from panel 2, full lift at the reveal in panel 5, soften under your sincere lines in panels 9 to 12, button at the end.

Captions: burned in, big, lowercase, one line at a time, synced to delivery. The video must fully land on mute.

Color: warm and clean on your face, neutral and crisp on the screen recordings so the product looks sharp.

---

## the post that carries it (same playbook as the script file)

Main tweet, capability first, no link. "you can now see exactly who is on your website. introducing beam. it finds out who your anonymous visitors are, matches their x handle, and drafts the reply so you say hi on their feed in 30 seconds." Then the native video.

First reply, link plus question. "private beta is open, limited spots, i onboard everyone myself. waitlist, getbeam.fyi. honest question, who is the last website visitor you actually talked to?"

Post mid morning US eastern on a tuesday to thursday, then reply to every comment for the first hour. Pin the 1:30 version, push the 45 second cut into the feed.

---

## one honest note

This founder story version will convert better with people who already trust you and land softer in a cold feed than the bolder concepts, because it asks for 30 seconds of attention before the payoff. The fix is the hook in panel 1. If that first line is delivered with real feeling, not read, the whole thing works. If it feels scripted, the algorithm will see the drop off in the first three seconds and bury it. Shoot panel 1 ten times and keep the most human take.
