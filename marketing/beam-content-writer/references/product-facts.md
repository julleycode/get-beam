## What Beam is

Person-level website visitor identification + outreach loop, built for indie founders. Tagline: "see who's into you. say hi back."

The loop (3 steps, this IS the product story):
1. **See them** — live list of who's on your site: names, companies, what page they're reading right now.
2. **Find them** — social handles auto-matched: LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Instagram, Slack, Facebook, 10+ platforms, real-time. User picks which platforms match their ICP.
3. **Charm them** — AI drafts outreach based on the visitor's recent posts and your product context, in your voice. User edits if wanted, sends manually from their own account in one click.

## Key numbers and claims (safe to publish)

- Identifies **60–80% of visitors on average** (multiple identification layers + LLM fallback enrichment; higher than typical reverse-IP tools; B2B traffic skews to the top of the range). ALWAYS quote as a range.

## Integrations

- Works on any site that can paste one line of HTML: WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Framer, Carrd, Ghost, Bubble, Next.js, React, Vue, Astro, plain HTML.

## Compliance and safety (big differentiator, use confidently)

- GDPR and CCPA compliant. Data never resold or shared.
- Never logs into the user's accounts; no fake profiles; zero automation on the user's side — every send is a manual click from native LinkedIn/X. So no LinkedIn ban risk.
- Doesn't scrape data the user couldn't see themselves.

## Competitor cheat sheet (VERIFY PRICING FRESH BEFORE PUBLISHING — this ages fast)

- **RB2B** — person-level ID, US traffic only, identify-only (pushes to Slack, you handle outreach), strong brand via Adam Robinson on LinkedIn. Beam angles: whole loop vs identify-only, price, non-US coverage (verify Beam's coverage claim with founder before stating).
- **Warmly** — warm intent + orchestration, priced for sales teams (entry commonly hundreds/mo — verify). Beam angle: founder pricing vs sales-team pricing.
- **Leadfeeder / Dealfront** — COMPANY-level only (shows firm names, not people), starts around $99/mo (verify). Beam angle: person vs company level; a company name isn't someone you can say hi to.
- **Clearbit Reveal** — absorbed into HubSpot (Breeze Intelligence); standalone product sunset. Beam angle: migration destination for ex-Clearbit users.
- **Clay** — enrichment/ops platform, powerful but setup-heavy. Beam angle: no-ops.
- **Snitcher, Leadinfo, Albacross, Visitor Queue, Lead Forensics** — company-level trackers, mostly EU/agency market. Same person-vs-company angle.
