# Squarespace Visitor Identification: See Your Visitors

Squarespace sites belong disproportionately to businesses where a single visitor matters: consultants, agencies, photographers, therapists, boutique services, and small firms whose entire pipeline is a services page and a contact form. Squarespace Analytics will tell you that 41 people visited this week; it will never tell you that one of them was the marketing director who read your services page twice and didn't fill the form. Squarespace visitor identification adds that missing layer, and the install is one paste into code injection.

## What Squarespace Analytics Can and Can't Do

The built-in analytics is genuinely decent at its job: traffic, sources, popular content, sales funnels for commerce plans. Its job just isn't identity, no analytics tool's is, and for a services business that's the expensive gap: roughly 97 percent of visitors leave without converting a form, and among them are the exact prospects comparing you against two other shortlisted providers this week. The identification methods that recover them, reverse IP for companies and identity graphs for people, are covered in our [how-it-works guide](https://getbeam.fyi/blog/identify-anonymous-website-visitors).

## The Install: Code Injection, Two Minutes

[Beam](https://getbeam.fyi/) installs on Squarespace through the standard code injection flow. In your dashboard: Settings, then Developer Tools (or Advanced on older plans), then Code Injection, and paste the Beam snippet into the Header field. Save. That applies site-wide, survives template changes, and requires no per-page work. Squarespace note: code injection requires a Business plan or higher, which most commercial Squarespace sites already run. (On a Personal plan, a per-page code block in the footer works as the fallback.)

Within about 30 minutes the first identified visitor appears in your Beam feed with an email alert: name, role, company, the pages they viewed, and matched social profiles across LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and 10+ platforms. ## FAQ

**Can you see who visits your Squarespace site?**
Not with built-in analytics, which reports anonymous aggregates. An identification snippet added via code injection resolves a majority of visitors to real names; Beam publishes a 60 to 80 percent average.

**How do I add visitor identification to Squarespace?**
Settings, Developer Tools, Code Injection, paste the snippet in Header, save. Requires a Business plan or higher; total time about two minutes plus 30 minutes to the first identified visitor.

**Does visitor identification slow down a Squarespace site?**
No; the script is asynchronous and analytics-pixel weight, with no effect on templates or rendering.

**Is there a free option for Squarespace visitor identification?**
Beam's free plan identifies 10 visitors a month at the person level, with social profiles and drafted outreach included, no card required.
