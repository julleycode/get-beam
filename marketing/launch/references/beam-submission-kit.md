## Per-platform field notes

### SaaSHub (dofollow) — `saashub.com/services/submit`
- MUST fill the **competitors/alternatives** field (RB2B, Leadfeeder, Clearbit) or the listing drops to the bottom of the review queue.
- Verify ownership after submit to raise priority. 1–5 day review.

### Indie Hackers (dofollow) — `indiehackers.com/new-product`
- Pair the product page with a genuine **launch/story post** ("I built Beam to see who visits my site"), not a bare link.

### Launching Next (dofollow, permanent) — `launchingnext.com/submit/`
- Straight form; free moderation queue can take weeks. Use the one-liner + long description as-is.

### Crunchbase (nofollow, AI-entity signal) — Create Profile
- Keep it strictly tech/SaaS framed. Fill founded date, HQ, 3–5 industries, founder, socials.

### AlternativeTo (nofollow, ChatGPT-alternative feed) — `alternativeto.net/manage/new/`
- ⚠️ 1-week cooldown after account creation before you can submit. **Create the account first, submit later.**

### Trustpilot (nofollow, trust) — `business.trustpilot.com/signup`
- Free = review management. Claim `trustpilot.com/review/getbeam.fyi`.

### G2 (link disputed, B2B due-diligence) — `sell.g2.com/create-a-profile`
- B2B-only. Free profiles can't display Grid badges (post-2025 change). Ask 5–10 real users for reviews via email/founder wall — never buy reviews.

### Capterra / GetApp (link disputed, Gartner network) — `capterra.com/vendors/`
- One vendor registration covers Capterra + GetApp + Software Advice. Must be packaged software in an existing category. High-quality screenshots required.
