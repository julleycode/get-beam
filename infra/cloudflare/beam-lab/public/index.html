Beam Lab

A simple public demo page for Beam.

What this page is

Beam helps website owners understand when AI assistants visit their site, and who may be behind those visits — questions that ordinary analytics usually leave unanswered.

This page is a lightweight demo environment operated by nhantown.com. It is not a sales page, and public signup is not open yet.

Privacy

This page does not set advertising cookies and does not sell data. When a browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, Beam treats the visit as opting out of identity lookup.
