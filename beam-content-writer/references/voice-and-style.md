# Beam Voice and Style

Beam runs two registers. Know which one you're writing in.

## Register 1: Product surfaces (homepage, landing pages, CTAs)

Lowercase, playful, punchy. Short lines. Dating-app metaphor ("see who's into you. say hi back.", "charm them", "watch the heart float up"). Self-aware humor ("take a nap. we'll beam the rest.", "no zapier duct tape"). Speaks to someone who just launched something ("so you launched your vibe-coded product? congrats. you rock!").

Use this register for: landing page copy, CTA blocks inside articles, social posts.

## Register 2: Blog articles (the default for this skill)

Title Case headlines. Professional but never corporate. The existing blog reads like a smart operator explaining exactly what to do:

- **Practical over theoretical.** "A practical, no-theory setup guide." Every section should let the reader DO something.
- **Numbers up front.** "Most B2B sites convert 1–3% of traffic." "five-touch, multi-channel." Concrete beats vague, always.
- **Second person, direct.** "Here's how to pick the right signal, timing, channel, message, and cadence."
- **Short sentences. Confident verbs.** If a sentence can lose a clause and mean the same, cut the clause.
- **Honest about tradeoffs.** Admitting a competitor's strength or Beam's limits builds the trust that converts.

## Hard bans (these read as AI slop or corporate filler)

- Banned phrases: "in today's digital landscape", "in the ever-evolving world of", "game-changer", "unlock the power of", "seamlessly", "leverage" (as a verb), "delve", "elevate your", "revolutionize", "it's important to note", "at the end of the day".
- No em dashes in article body text. Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead.
- No walls of bullet points. Bullets only when listing genuinely parallel items; otherwise write prose.
- No fake urgency ("act now!"), no exclamation marks in headings.
- Never start an article with a dictionary definition ("Visitor identification is defined as..."). Start with the reader's situation or a number.

## Word choices

- "founders", "makers", "your site", "your product" — not "businesses", "organizations", "enterprises".
- "visitors" or "people" — not "prospects" or "leads" until they're actually identified.
- "say hi", "reach out", "reply" — not "engage in outreach motions".
- Beam is always lowercase "beam" in product-register copy; "Beam" capitalized in blog prose is fine.

## Article rhythm

Hook (2–4 sentences, the reader's pain or a sharp number) → promise (what this page will give them) → deliver in scannable H2 sections → honest FAQ → single CTA block in product register. One CTA mid-article max, one at the end. Never three.
