faq

questions you're probably about to ask.

when will i see my first identified visitor? +

within 30 minutes of installing the snippet. you'll get an email the moment beam catches one.

how accurate is identification? +

60 to 80 percent of visitors on average. we run multiple identification layers with llm fallback enrichment, which pushes match rate higher than typical reverse-ip tools. b2b traffic skews to the top of that range.

which platforms does beam cover? +

linkedin, x, instagram, slack, facebook, and 10+ more. real-time across all of them. you pick which platforms matter most for your icp.

does beam work with wix, wordpress, or shopify? +

yes — plus squarespace, webflow, framer, carrd, ghost, next.js, plain html... if your builder lets you paste one line of html (they all do), beam works. from there you can sync leads to hubspot, salesforce, or pipedrive, and export ad audiences for meta, google, and linkedin.

do i have to write the engagement copy myself? +

no. beam drafts it based on their recent posts and your product context. you edit if you want, then send from your own account in one click.

how is this different from clay or rb2b? +

clay needs setup and ops know-how. rb2b just identifies — you do the rest. beam does the whole loop. paste url, done.

will this get my linkedin banned? +

no. beam never logs into your account. you click send manually from native linkedin every time. zero automation on your side.

how compliant is the data? +

we only automate through your own account, never fake profiles. we don't scrape data you couldn't see yourself. your data stays yours — we don't sell or share it. gdpr and ccpa compliant.

can i cancel anytime? +

yes. 2 clicks from the dashboard. no contracts, no "talk to sales" gate.
