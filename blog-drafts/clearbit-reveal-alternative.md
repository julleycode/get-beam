# Clearbit Reveal Alternative: What to Use in 2026

If you're looking for a Clearbit Reveal alternative, it's probably because Reveal, as a standalone product, no longer exists. HubSpot acquired Clearbit and folded it into Breeze Intelligence, and the sunset rolled out in stages: Clearbit Connect shut down in December 2024, the free Clearbit platform including the legacy Reveal tools ended on April 30, 2025, and the free Logo API followed on December 1, 2025.

What used to be Reveal now lives inside HubSpot as a credit-based add-on. If you're a HubSpot shop, that might be fine. If you're not, you've been quietly locked out of a tool you built workflows around. Here's what the landscape looks like in 2026 and what to use instead.

**TL;DR:** Reveal's replacement, HubSpot Breeze Intelligence, requires a paid HubSpot subscription (Sales Hub Pro and above for the visitor identification features) plus credits starting around $45 a month for 100, and it identifies companies, not people. If you use Salesforce, Pipedrive, Attio, or no CRM at all, or you want person-level identification, you need a different tool. Beam does person-level identification with built-in outreach from $0, and works with any CRM.

## What Happened to Clearbit Reveal

Reveal did one thing well: it took an anonymous visitor's IP address and told you which company was on your site. Thousands of teams wired it into their routing, alerting, and personalization. Then the acquisition changed the terms.

As of 2026, the practical situation is this. Website visitor identification, the old Reveal capability, is a HubSpot Breeze Intelligence feature. It requires a paid HubSpot subscription, with the Reveal-style features tied to Sales Hub Pro and above. Usage runs on HubSpot credits, starting around $45 a month for 100 credits, shared across HubSpot's usage-based AI tools, resetting monthly with no rollover. And it's HubSpot-only: there is no path to use it with Salesforce, Pipedrive, Close, or a webhook into your own stack.

To be fair, if you're already paying for Sales Hub Pro and live inside HubSpot, Breeze is a reasonable consolidation. The data quality heritage is real, and native integration has value. This page isn't for those teams. It's for everyone Reveal left behind.

## What the Old Reveal Users Actually Need

Three requirements come up again and again from ex-Reveal users, and they're worth naming before evaluating any alternative. CRM independence: your visitor data should land wherever you work, not inside one vendor's walled garden. Predictable cost: credit systems that reset monthly make budgeting a guessing game. And increasingly, person-level identification: knowing "Acme Corp visited" was the 2023 standard. Knowing which human at Acme read your pricing page is what makes outreach possible.
