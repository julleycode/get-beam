# Beam vs Warmly: $19 Tool or $15K Platform?

We make Beam, so read this with the usual skepticism reserved for vendor comparisons. The good news for both of us: Beam vs Warmly is one of the easiest calls in this category, because the two products barely compete. They identify the same signal and sell it to completely different buyers.

**TL;DR:** Warmly is a go-to-market orchestration platform for sales teams, with paid plans starting around $15,000 a year as of July 2026. Beam is a visitor identification and outreach loop for founders, from $0 to $49 a month. If you have a RevOps function, evaluate Warmly. If you ARE the RevOps function, and also the CEO and the SDR, Beam was built for you.

## What Warmly Is

Warmly de-anonymizes site visitors at company and contact level, then orchestrates everything downstream: automated email and LinkedIn sequences, AI chat that engages visitors live, meeting booking, dynamic pop-up offers, retargeting sync, and intent data layered on top. Its free tier reveals up to 500 visitors a month, one of the most generous in the category, though without the automation.

The pricing matches the ambition. Public pricing research from 2026 puts the entry tier around $15,000 a year, with on-site person-level identification and AI chat in a tier around $30,000, and custom enterprise pricing above that. For a mid-market sales org that will genuinely use orchestration, that math can work. Our [Warmly alternative guide](https://getbeam.fyi/blog/warmly-alternative) covers the full pricing picture.

## What Beam Is

Beam does the loop one founder actually runs: see who's on your site (person-level, with a published average of 60 to 80 percent of visitors identified), see where they're active (auto-matched profiles across LinkedIn, X, and 10+ platforms), and send them a reply Beam drafted in your voice, manually, from your own account. One line of HTML to install, roughly 30 minutes to the first identified visitor, and flat pricing: $0 for 10 identified visitors a month, $19 for 50, $49 for unlimited. How person-level identification works, and why it beats company-level for direct outreach, is covered in our [category guide](https://getbeam.fyi/blog/person-level-visitor-identification).

## The Fit Question Nobody Asks

The real comparison isn't features, it's staffing. Warmly's value assumes someone owns the platform: configuring ICP tiers, maintaining sequences, watching dashboards. That person costs more than the software. If that person exists at your company, Warmly's depth is usable and this comparison is genuinely competitive. If that person doesn't exist, every orchestration feature you're paying five figures for becomes shelf-ware, and the honest comparison collapses to: which tool gets a founder from "someone's on my pricing page" to "I just sent them a good message" fastest. That's the lane Beam was built for, and the same logic applies against other team-scale tools in our [Beam vs RB2B comparison](https://getbeam.fyi/blog/beam-vs-rb2b).

## FAQ

**Is Beam a Warmly alternative?**
For founders and small teams, yes: it covers the identification and outreach part of Warmly's stack at $0 to $49 a month. For sales orgs that need orchestration, AI chat, and intent data, Warmly is a different class of product.

**How much does Warmly cost compared to Beam?**
Public 2026 research puts Warmly's paid plans from roughly $15,000 a year. Beam's plans are $0, $19, and $49 a month, published on the pricing page, no sales call required.

**Does Warmly have a free plan?**
Yes, and it's generous: up to 500 revealed visitors a month at company and contact level, without automation. It's a good way to sample the category alongside Beam's free plan.

**Which has better visitor identification, Beam or Warmly?**
Both publish their own claims and measure differently. Beam reports 60 to 80 percent of visitors identified on average. The reliable answer is running both free tiers on your own traffic for two weeks and counting.
