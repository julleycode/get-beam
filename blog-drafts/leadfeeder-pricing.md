# Leadfeeder Pricing (2026): What You'll Actually Pay

Leadfeeder pricing has one number everyone quotes (€99 a month) and several numbers that decide what you actually pay: the 40 percent monthly-billing premium, the volume tiers above the entry price, and the 7-day data window on the free plan. Here's the full picture as of July 2026. (Transparency: we make Beam, a competitor in this category. Figures below come from Dealfront's published pricing and independent research; confirm current numbers on their site.)

## Leadfeeder Plans at a Glance (July 2026)

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | €0 | Up to 100 identified companies/mo, 7-day data retention, unlimited users |
| Paid (entry) | €99/mo billed annually | Up to 50 identified companies/mo, unlimited data storage, firmographics, employee contact details, CRM integrations |
| Paid (volume tiers) | Scales to €1,199/mo annually | Higher identification volumes, up to 20,001–40,000 companies/mo |
| Monthly billing | +40% (entry ≈ €165/mo) | Same features, no annual commitment |
| Dealfront Platform | €399/mo | Adds the Dealfront B2B database (60M companies, 400M contacts) |

Two structural notes. Annual billing is charged 12 months upfront, so the real entry commitment is roughly €1,188, not €99. And yes, the free plan identifies more companies (100) than the entry paid plan (50); what you're buying at €99 is retention beyond 7 days, contact details, and integrations, not raw volume.

## The Costs Worth Weighing

**The retention trap on free.** Seven days of data means no historical analysis, no spotting the account that visited monthly all quarter. The free plan is a demo, and it's designed to feel like one.

**The annual lock.** The 40 percent monthly premium pushes most buyers to annual, which is fine if the tool proves out and painful if it doesn't. Budget the €1,188 upfront, not the €99 sticker.

**Company-level means homework remains.** Leadfeeder tells you which company visited; identifying the right human, and reaching them, is your workflow. The contact database helps, but it's lookup, not identification: it doesn't know which employee was actually on your site. ## How Leadfeeder Pricing Compares

For account-based teams wanting company-level intelligence with strong European coverage and CRM depth, Leadfeeder's pricing is defensible and its Dealfront platform tier adds real database value. Against person-level tools the comparison changes shape: RB2B starts at $79 a month for individual identification (US traffic only), and Beam runs $0 to $49 flat for person-level identification with matched social profiles and AI-drafted outreach included. If the reason you want visitor data is to actually contact the visitor, our [Beam vs Leadfeeder breakdown](https://getbeam.fyi/blog/beam-vs-leadfeeder) covers which shape fits which team, and the [full tools roundup](https://getbeam.fyi/blog/best-website-visitor-identification-software) puts all eight options side by side.

## Is Leadfeeder Worth It?

For a mid-market team running account-based sales into Europe with a CRM-centered workflow: often yes, especially at annual pricing with real traffic volume. For a founder or small team that wants to know who visited and message them personally: you'd be paying €1,188 upfront for company names and doing the person-finding yourself. Run the free tiers of one company-level and one person-level tool for two weeks and count which produced conversations, not just records.

## FAQ

**How much does Leadfeeder cost?**
As of July 2026: free plan (100 companies/mo, 7-day retention), paid from €99/mo billed annually (about €165 month-to-month), scaling by volume to €1,199/mo. The Dealfront Platform tier at €399/mo adds their B2B contact database.

**Is Leadfeeder free?**
There's a permanent free plan, limited to 100 identified companies a month with 7-day data retention. It shows you the product but isn't built to run a sales process on.

**Why does Leadfeeder cost more monthly than annually?**
Monthly billing carries roughly a 40 percent premium (about €165 vs €99 at entry). Annual plans are charged 12 months upfront.

**What's a cheaper Leadfeeder alternative?**
Depends what you need. Snitcher does company-level from €49/mo. Beam does person-level identification with outreach drafting from $0 to $49/mo flat, which covers the "actually contact the visitor" use case company-level tools leave to you.
